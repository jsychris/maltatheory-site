Malta Theory

Support

Malta Theory is a study app for Malta's driving theory test — car and motorcycle.

Common questions

  • How do I switch study mode? Profile tab → Study mode (Car/Motorcycle).
  • How do I reset my progress? Profile tab → Reset all progress. This only affects your device — the app stores nothing anywhere else.
  • Is this an official Transport Malta app? No. It is an independent study aid based on the Highway Code (Malta) and Maltese road law. Rules change — always check transport.gov.mt before your test.

Report a problem or a content error

Spotted a wrong answer, a typo, or a bug? Please email drivingtheoryapps@gmail.com with the question text or a screenshot — content corrections are treated as the highest priority and ship in the next update.
